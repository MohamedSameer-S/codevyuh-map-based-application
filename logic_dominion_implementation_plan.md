# Sprint 2C: Logic Dominion Campus Master Plan

*Vision: A prestigious, tranquil, and heavily structured university campus centered around the pursuit of knowledge. The design philosophy dictates that nature is curated, architecture is monumental, and all roads lead to learning.*

---

## 1. The Academy (Primary Landmark)
* **Role:** The undisputed heart of the civilization; the seat of higher learning and governance.
* **Ideal Distance:** Absolute Center (0 units).
* **Relationship:** Acts as the gravitational anchor. All paths, sightlines, and lesser structures orient toward it.
* **Visual Contribution:** Its massive footprint and towering height immediately establish the player's arrival in Logic Dominion. It is the defining, unmissable silhouette of the biome.

## 2. The Grand Library (Secondary Landmark)
* **Role:** The archive of the civilization; a quiet, monumental structure for research and reflection.
* **Ideal Distance:** 500 - 800 units.
* **Relationship:** Set back from the immediate bustle of the Academy core. It commands its own dedicated district but functionally serves the Academy.
* **Visual Contribution:** Its vast footprint (~1050px) and glowing cyan crystals provide a secondary visual anchor. It balances the map's visual weight, ensuring the civilization looks expansive rather than concentrated in a single building.

## 3. The Monument Plaza (Civic Focal Point)
* **Role:** A place of public gathering, honoring the founders of logic and science.
* **Ideal Distance:** 200 - 400 units.
* **Relationship:** Sits directly between the Academy and the outer zones, acting as a grand gateway or checkpoint into the inner sanctum.
* **Visual Contribution:** The tall, sharp, vertical obelisk cuts through the horizontal nature of the ground plazas, providing a crucial vertical sightline that effortlessly guides the player's eye toward the Academy.

## 4. The Fountain Plaza (Tranquil Hub)
* **Role:** A space of kinetic energy and tranquility; the social crossroads of the campus.
* **Ideal Distance:** 150 - 250 units.
* **Relationship:** Located in the immediate shadow of the Academy. It sits at the intersection of the main walking paths.
* **Visual Contribution:** The bright cyan water and dynamic circular geometry break up the rigid, imposing marble architecture, adding life and movement to the otherwise static stone environment.

## 5. Main Civic Plaza (The Grand Concourse)
* **Role:** The vast, paved central artery connecting the primary structures.
* **Ideal Distance:** 50 - 300 units (Spanning outward from the Academy).
* **Relationship:** The mortar that binds the Academy, Fountain, and Monument together into a single cohesive "Campus."
* **Visual Contribution:** Creates crucial "negative space." By paving over the raw terrain with clean stone, it tells the player this is a highly developed, curated urban environment, making the hero buildings pop via stark contrast.

## 6. Courtyard Spaces (Academic Cloisters)
* **Role:** Smaller, intimate paved areas for students and scholars.
* **Ideal Distance:** 300 - 600 units.
* **Relationship:** Satellites branching off the Main Civic Plaza. They tuck securely near the Library or behind the Academy.
* **Visual Contribution:** Provides density and architectural noise at the mid-scale. They will house the medium props (knowledge pillars, pedestals) and prevent the campus from feeling like giant buildings floating in an empty void.

## 7. Garden Areas (Curated Nature)
* **Role:** Highly maintained, geometric green spaces offering peace and fresh air.
* **Ideal Distance:** 100 - 500 units (Interspersed among plazas).
* **Relationship:** Borders the plazas, courtyards, and paths, acting as a natural buffer between stone districts.
* **Visual Contribution:** Introduces vibrant, contrasting organic color (emerald greens, bright flowers) against the stark white/cyan marble. It communicates control over nature (perfect hedges vs wild forests).

## 8. Walking Paths (The Veins)
* **Role:** Pristine paved walkways guiding traffic between all zones.
* **Ideal Distance:** 0 - 1000 units.
* **Relationship:** Connects the wild outer dirt roads directly to the inner plazas, the Library, and the courtyards.
* **Visual Contribution:** Draws literal geometric lines on the map that guide the player's eye directly to the points of interest, enhancing the feeling of an organized, premeditated city.

## 9. Natural Biome Transition (The Frontier)
* **Role:** The boundary where civilization ends and the wild plains begin.
* **Ideal Distance:** 800 - 1200+ units.
* **Relationship:** Surrounds the entire campus. Here, stone plazas slowly fracture into dirt paths, and curated gardens give way to wild, scattered procedural forests.
* **Visual Contribution:** Creates a beautiful gradient of density. The stark contrast between the wild, chaotic trees on the perimeter and the rigid, mathematical marble of the core beautifully emphasizes the core theme of "Logic."
